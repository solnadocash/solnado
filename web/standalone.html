SolnaDoCash enables private SOL transfers on Solana using the PrivacyCash shielded pool.

How it works:

  1. Your SOL is deposited into a privacy pool
  2. The pool sends funds to the recipient
  3. No on-chain link between you and the recipient

Privacy Model:
The shielded pool uses zero-knowledge proofs to break the transaction graph. Your deposit and the recipient's withdrawal cannot be linked on-chain.

Fees:
~0.8% total (0.35% + 0.006 SOL protocol + 0.001 SOL relayer). Recipient gets exact amount you enter.
